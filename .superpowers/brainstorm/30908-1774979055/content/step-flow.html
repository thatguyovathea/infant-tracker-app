Proposed 7-Step Walkthrough Flow

Each step is a tooltip/modal pointing at a specific UI element. Does this sequence feel right?

Walkthrough Sequence
1
Welcome Modal
Full-screen overlay. App icon + "Welcome to Care Tracking" + brief tagline. "Let's take a quick tour" button.
2
Quick-Log Buttons
Tooltip pointing at Eat/Sleep/Change circles. "One tap to log a feeding, sleep, or diaper change."
3
Sleep Toggle
Tooltip pointing at Sleep button. "Tap once to start sleep, tap again to end it. We'll track the duration."
4
Activity Drawer
Tooltip pointing at "Recent Activity" handle. "Swipe up to see today's activity log."
5
Bottom Navigation
Tooltip pointing at bottom bar icons. "Family, activity log, barcode scanner, and notifications — all one tap away."
6
Weekly Charts
Tooltip pointing at sparkline charts. "Your baby's trends at a glance. Tap a chart for more detail."
7
Settings & You're Ready
Tooltip pointing at gear icon. "Customize themes, defaults, and more in Settings. You're all set!" Final step dismisses the walkthrough.

Every step has a "Next" button and a "Skip tour" link. Progress dots at the bottom (● ● ● ● ● ● ●).
